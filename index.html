How TLC works

Simple, clear and safe.

30-Day Pilot Program

We’re testing the service with selected partner venues and families, gathering real feedback to build a reliable system before wider expansion.

  1. 1

    Parent books

    Date, venue, child details and service duration are shared.

  2. 2

    TLC confirms

    Availability, staff assignment, service terms and applicable fee are confirmed.

  3. 3

    Child check-in

    Parent completes handover and check-in with authorised TLC staff.

  4. 4

    Supervised care

    TLC staff provide the agreed service within the designated area.

  5. 5

    Safe handover

    Child is released only to the parent or authorised pick-up person.

Our approach
to safety

Pilot operations are built around verified, authorised staff, clear parent consent, emergency contacts, defined start and end times, and venue-specific rules. Care is provided only within the agreed scope and designated area.
